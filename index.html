Who We Are

A Foundation Built on Compassion

Uplift Relief Foundation is a grassroots NGO based in Nagpur dedicated to rescuing street animals and empowering underprivileged children through free education and life skills.

Founded by young changemakers in 2026, we believe compassion — when backed by relentless action — transforms entire communities.
